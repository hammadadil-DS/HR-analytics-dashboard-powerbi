# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence solution developed in **Power BI** to transform raw HR data into actionable workforce insights. The dashboard enables HR professionals and business leaders to monitor employee attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, compensation trends, and organizational performance through an intuitive, data-driven interface.

Designed using best practices in data modeling, Power Query, and DAX, this project demonstrates how HR data can be leveraged to support strategic decision-making and improve employee retention.

---

## Business Problem

Employee attrition directly impacts organizational productivity and hiring costs. HR teams often struggle to identify the factors driving employee turnover due to scattered or unstructured data.

This dashboard addresses that challenge by consolidating HR metrics into a single analytical platform, allowing stakeholders to identify trends, monitor KPIs, and make informed workforce decisions.

---

## Objectives

* Analyze employee attrition across departments and job roles.
* Monitor workforce demographics and diversity.
* Evaluate job satisfaction and employee engagement.
* Track compensation and tenure-related trends.
* Provide interactive reports for HR decision-making.
